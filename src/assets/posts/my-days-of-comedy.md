I have been struggling lately. Several aspects of my life have been falling apart pretty much at the same time. At first, I was really upset, as it is expected and normal. But after a while, the situation was quite ridiculous...

# Laughter as a cover up

If you get a flat tire and your fridge gets broken, it is really anoying and frustrating. But then if you catch the flu at the same time... stuff just gets funnier and funnier.

In my case, big life changing events where happening at the same time and I was suffering a lot. In order to find a way to cope with it I thought... _"well, I guess six months from now, I will laugh at this"_. Then it hit me... I am going to voluntarily remove that six months (or wathever) period. Why not start laughing right now?

So there I was, laughing. 

In general terms, I think laughter is a powerful tool. If you laugh at things that are scary, they lose part of their power. I am not saying that if you get diagnosed with cancer, starting laughing will make everything ok. It won't. But it will make it a **little bit** better.

It worked... for a while. Laughter is only a cover up. It was really useful, for sure. A new tool in my tool box that I am sure I will use again in the future. But covering up is never enough. It can save you when you are drawning, and you should do wathever works to breathe again.

Soon I realised that if I wanted to find a permanent solution, with stable future consequences, I needed to find more mechanisms.
